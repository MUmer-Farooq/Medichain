Features

Everything Healthcare Needs

A complete, secure platform built for modern healthcare interoperability.

Blockchain Security

Every record is cryptographically hashed and stored on Hyperledger Fabric — tampering is impossible.

Multi-Hospital Access

Authorized hospitals can securely access and update patient records with full consent management.

Patient Consent Control

Patients retain full control over who can access their data with time-limited permissions.

Complete Medical Records

Doctors create comprehensive records including diagnosis, prescriptions, lab results and reports.

Real-time Analytics

Comprehensive dashboards with live statistics, charts, and reports for administrators.

Role-Based Access

Strict RBAC for System Admin, Hospital Admin, Doctor, and Patient with Flask-Login sessions.

Process

How MediChain Works

1
Hospital Registration

Hospitals apply for membership. System Admin reviews and approves, granting network access.

2
Doctor Onboarding

Hospital Admins register doctors who receive credentials to access and create patient records.

3
Record Creation

Doctors create medical records that are hashed and committed to the Hyperledger Fabric blockchain.

4
Secure Access

Patients control access. Authorized doctors from any member hospital can view records with consent.
